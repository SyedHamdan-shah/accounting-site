📜 Corporate Accounting Policies & Governance
IFRS & US GAAP Compliance Principles governing Accumen Pro

1. Revenue Recognition (IFRS 15 / ASC 606)

Revenue is recognized upon satisfaction of performance obligations when control of goods or services is transferred to the customer. All invoiced revenues are recorded net of discounts and sales taxes.

2. Property, Plant & Equipment (IAS 16 / ASC 360)

Fixed assets are capitalized at acquisition cost less accumulated depreciation. Depreciation is computed systematically over useful asset lives (Straight-Line or Declining-Balance) down to salvage value.

3. Accrual Basis of Accounting (IAS 1)

Transactions are recorded in the periods in which they occur, regardless of when cash is received or disbursed. Prepayments and accrued expenses are amortized using standard monthly amortization schedules.

4. Segregation of Duties & Dual Control

Routine operations are recorded by the Bookkeeper and formally reviewed, validated, and closed by the Chief Accountant to guarantee internal controls and zero financial fraud.
